# Towards leadership's love for AI

Everything is AI today. AI driven Finance, agentic CRM, agentic HR, AI Sales agent etc.

I am seeing two reasons why leadership wants to use AI. First one is fairly simple, there is too much momentum, all their peers are talking about, there is a Fear of missing out. But the second one is interesting thought process. The other class of leadership loves AI because they thing, they can control the process, removing human the from the equation gives them control and predictability. They think this is more scalable. But whats missing is that LLM and agents are just tokens and words put together. There is creative thinking, ideation built in.
